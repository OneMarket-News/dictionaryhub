# Coverage and exclusions

BibleRoot coverage is exact English edition text for Genesis 1, Psalm 23, Ecclesiastes 3, and John 1 in KJV, ASV, WEB, and YLT. The only matched field is `exactText`.

HistoryRoot coverage contains 628 released public contextual records from `historyroot-plymouth-knowledge-dataset-v1` 1.3.0. Fields vary by record kind: labels; names and published aliases; descriptions; date labels and notes; account content and publication labels; claim statements; evidence explanations; interpretation text and uncertainty; relationship/causal explanations and uncertainty; cultural-memory narrative and period label. IDs, hashes, governance/audit material, hidden metadata, drafts, and rejected proposals are excluded.

DictionaryRoot coverage contains all 500 accepted, non-fixture lemmas in `dictionaryroot-core-lexical-corpus-v1` 1.0.0. Senses, definition claims, and lexical relationships do not participate.

Excluded entirely: BibleRoot Original Language, morphology, commentary, rights/provenance prose, direct BibleRoot-to-HistoryRoot links, entity equivalence, semantic scoring, influence, causation, theological significance, agreement, and contradiction. Every production row is `textually_observed` and `unreviewed`.
