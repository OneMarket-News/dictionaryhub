## Why These Concepts Come First

These concepts are the foundation of SourceRoot because they explain how knowledge becomes trustworthy.

SourceRoot is not just storing words. It is storing:

- What a concept means
- Where the concept came from
- What supports the concept
- What the concept connects to
- Why those relationships exist
- How the concept changed over time

These 25 concepts describe the system itself.

---

## Batch 1: Core Knowledge Concepts

### Truth

Truth is the quality of a claim, belief, statement, or understanding being aligned with reality, fact, or verified support.

### Knowledge

Knowledge is organized understanding that can be justified, communicated, revised, and connected.

### Evidence

Evidence is support used to evaluate whether a claim, belief, or statement should be accepted.

### Verification

Verification is the process of checking whether something is accurate, reliable, or supported.

### Source

A source is the origin or reference point from which information, evidence, or a claim comes.

---

## Batch 2: Information Concepts

### Reference

A reference is a cited pointer to a source, work, document, or authority.

### Data

Data are recorded facts, observations, measurements, or symbols that can be stored, processed, or interpreted.

### Information

Information is data given structure, context, or meaning.

### Meaning

Meaning is the significance, interpretation, or intended sense of a word, sign, claim, or concept.

### Context

Context is the surrounding condition, background, or frame that affects how something is understood.

---

## Batch 3: Claim and Trust Concepts

### Claim

A claim is a statement that asserts something to be true or valid.

### Fact

A fact is something that is the case or can be verified as true.

### Belief

A belief is something accepted as true by a person or group, whether or not it is verified.

### Trust

Trust is confidence in the reliability, accuracy, integrity, or truthfulness of something.

### Provenance

Provenance is the origin, history, custody, or source trail of something.

---

## Batch 4: Validation Concepts

### Origin

Origin is the starting point or source from which something begins.

### Revision

A revision is a recorded change made to an entry, claim, definition, or relationship.

### Authority

Authority is a person, institution, source, or system treated as credible or reliable in a domain.

### Reliability

Reliability is the degree to which something can be depended on to be accurate or consistent.

### Validity

Validity is the degree to which a claim, argument, method, or measurement is logically or properly grounded.

---

## Batch 5: Interpretation and Structure Concepts

### Accuracy

Accuracy is closeness to truth, correctness, or the actual state of things.

### Interpretation

Interpretation is the act of assigning meaning, explanation, or understanding to information.

### Relationship

A relationship is a defined connection between two concepts, claims, sources, or objects.

### Definition

A definition is a statement that explains the meaning of a word, concept, or term.

### Concept

A concept is an abstract unit of meaning or understanding that can be named, described, connected, and revised.
