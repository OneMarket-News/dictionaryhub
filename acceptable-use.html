Acceptable Use Policy Draft

Protect the knowledge system from abuse

The platform includes moderation reports, account suspension, record locks, audit trails, and permission checks. This draft explains the conduct those controls are intended to support.

Pre-launch policy draftReplace example domains, contacts, retention periods, governing terms, and provider lists after legal and operational review.

Prohibited activity

Do not submit spam, malicious code, deceptive identities, harassment, unlawful content, fabricated citations, unauthorized copyrighted material, credential attacks, bulk scraping that harms availability, or attempts to bypass permissions.

Editorial integrity

Do not conceal conflicts of interest, manipulate ranking for promotion, remove contrary evidence without explanation, approve your own proposal when policy blocks self-approval, or use administrative access to rewrite provenance.

Enforcement

Authorized moderators may triage reports, lock records, suspend accounts, revoke sessions or roles, and preserve audit evidence. Final enforcement timelines and appeals should be established before public launch.
