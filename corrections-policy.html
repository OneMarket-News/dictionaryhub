Corrections and Disputes Policy Draft

Correct records without erasing history

DictionaryRoot is designed to preserve proposals, evidence, review decisions, publications, supersession, rollback, and moderation locks as separate records.

Pre-launch policy draftReplace example domains, contacts, retention periods, governing terms, and provider lists after legal and operational review.

Submitting a correction

A correction should identify the exact target, describe the proposed patch, disclose editorial interpretation, and attach supporting or contradicting SourceRoot evidence.

Review and publication

A contributor submits a draft; an authorized reviewer may request changes, approve, or reject it. Unless explicitly enabled by policy, a contributor cannot approve their own proposal. A publisher may publish only an approved proposal.

Disputes and locks

Moderators can lock a target while copyright, integrity, abuse, or conflict-of-interest concerns are reviewed. A lock blocks governed publication until released.

Rollback

A publication may be rolled back with a recorded reason. Rollback deactivates the current overlay and may restore the previous publication while retaining the full event trail.

Contact and timelines

A public correction address, response targets, appeal process, and emergency escalation path must be added before launch.
