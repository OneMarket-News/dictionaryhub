Privacy Notice Draft

Privacy choices and account data

This implementation-ready draft describes the data categories DictionaryRoot is designed to process. It is not a substitute for counsel review before public launch.

Pre-launch policy draftReplace example domains, contacts, retention periods, governing terms, and provider lists after legal and operational review.

Data associated with an account

DictionaryRoot may store a stable user ID, verified email address, display name, optional public handle, linked sign-in provider metadata, organization memberships, roles, permissions, active sessions, proposals, comments, decisions, publications, moderation reports, and audit events.

Why the system keeps provenance

Editorial and administrative actions are retained so published knowledge changes remain attributable and explainable. Account deletion removes sign-in identities and disables the account, while published and audit records may retain an anonymized user reference where integrity, security, or legal obligations require it.

Cookies and sessions

The governed platform uses an HTTP-only session cookie. Provider passwords are not collected by DictionaryRoot. OAuth credentials and email-delivery secrets remain on the backend.

User controls

Signed-in users can review linked identities and sessions, revoke sessions, download an account export, request deletion, and confirm account deletion from the Account experience.

Service providers and retention

Hosting, database, identity, email, monitoring, and backup providers should be listed here once selected. Retention periods, regional processing, legal bases, and contact details must be finalized before public use.
