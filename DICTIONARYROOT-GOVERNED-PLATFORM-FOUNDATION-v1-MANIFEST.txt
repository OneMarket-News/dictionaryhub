- Governed editorial proposals, evidence, comments, review decisions, publication overlays, rollback, and self-approval prevention
- Moderation reports, account suspension actions, publication record locks, and audit events
- Account, Workflow, and Administration customer experiences
- Local/staging/production environment examples, Docker templates, backup/restore tools, setup/start scripts, OpenAPI reference, policy drafts, and pilot checklist

Operational boundary:
The release implements provider adapters and deployment-ready configuration, but it does not contain live provider credentials, purchase hosting, create public domains, finalize legal policies, or deploy production infrastructure. Public login requires operator-supplied HTTPS domains, OAuth credentials, email delivery, PostgreSQL, secrets, migrations, backups, monitoring, and staging acceptance.
