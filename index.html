What is SourceRoot?

SourceRoot is not just a dictionary, Bible tool, wiki viewer, or graph demo. It is the layer underneath knowledge applications that asks what is being claimed, where it came from, how it is connected, how trustworthy it is, and whether it can be safely imported or cited.

Claim Layer

Assertions

SourceRoot separates a concept from the individual claims made about that concept.

Relationship Layer

Edges

Relationships are inspectable objects with their own source support and trust metadata.

Provenance Layer

Sources

Sources carry publisher, license, review, credibility, verification, and usage metadata.

Safety Layer

Import Validation

Structured knowledge is checked before it enters the system.

Current MVP: Light

The current MVP includes live imported knowledge across SourceRoot bundles. The Light demo remains the clearest cross-domain example, connecting physical, dictionary, biblical, symbolic, truth, knowledge, guidance, revelation, and visibility contexts.

The larger vision

SourceRoot is a trust layer for human and AI-readable knowledge. Instead of flattening information into uninspectable answers, it preserves the structure underneath: what was claimed, who or what supports it, how it connects, how confident the system is, and what still needs review.

The Light MVP is only the first proof. The same model can extend to dictionaries, scripture, history, technical documentation, product knowledge, legal references, public datasets, and future AI citation workflows.
